Deathtrip

Kjenner du fortsatt veien i fylla?

Deathtrip is a norwegian attitude campaign where schools all over the world compete to make the best campaign. The theme is to stop young people driving under the influence.

In my group, I had multiple roles. I played as an actor in our films, helped develop the core idea and produce a storyboard, as well as editing the main film and the SoMe film.

To the right you can see the two videos I edited. The top video is the main film we made which is 1 minute and 45 seconds long. The other video is a shorter edit of the main video made to be published on social media platforms such as TikTok and Instagram. It is just 15 seconds long but still maintains the core concept of our campaign.
